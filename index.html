Our Story

A legacy of passion, precision, and pursuit of perfection.

The Vision of Chef Laurent

Born in Lyon, France, Chef Laurent Beaumont discovered his passion for cuisine in his grandmother's kitchen at the age of seven. After training under some of the world's most celebrated chefs — including a formative period at Noma and The French Laundry — he opened Lumière in 2015 with a singular vision: to create dishes that transcend the plate and become lasting memories.

"Cooking is an act of love. Every ingredient has a story, every plate has a purpose. At Lumière, we don't just serve food — we create moments of wonder."

— Chef Laurent Beaumont
